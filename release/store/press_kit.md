## Description

Misty Packland is a tiny dark fantasy idle game that lives in a window on your desktop. Your island grows while you work:
day plants feed on your real activity at the computer, night plants bloom in the dark, and lamps burning Ember hold back
the mist after sunset. Out of the mist come a ghostly merchant, will-o'-wisps, falling stars and things from the deep.

## Features

- An idle farm that grows from your real work, with no clicking required
- One in-game day lasts an hour: light the island at night and choose which plots stay in the light
- About 20 crops plus fruit trees, hybrids, a swarm of helper wisps
- Expand the island into the mist and uncover a cemetery, a swamp and ruins
- Night events, a bestiary and a short story about the last lighthouse
- Borderless, always-on-top, corner-snapped, transparent or mini window; tray icon and notifications
- Fully offline. The game only reads how long ago you last moved the mouse or pressed a key, never what you type.
